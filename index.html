About Us

The purpose of this letter is to introduce our company General Security & Information Services Pvt. Ltd., which has been a leader in the field of Security provision to Central Govt./State Govt./ PSU organizations and Financial Institutions since last 11 years. We have catered to several organizations in the past and have had a good working relation with all of them, with no complaints from our clients. Our Organization is ISO 9001:2015 Certified, ISO 14001:2004 Certification for Environmental Support & Policy Management Systems. We are also OHSAS 18001 Certified Company. We are the INDIA’S FIRST DUN & BRADSTREET REGISTERED SECURITY SERVICE & MECHANIZED CLEANING COMPANY.

We are the Mechanized Cleaning Conservancy company in India who has been awarded with International Quality Summit Awards in GOLD Category in the IQS 2015 held at New York. We have highly structured organization with streamlined working procedures in our company with separate dedicated Departments i.e. Billing Deptt., Statutory Deptt., Tax Deptt., Error Auditing & Rectification Deptt., Administration Deptt., Legal Deptt., Immediate Crisis Handling Deptt., Documents Procurement & Validation Deptt., Tender Deptt., Surveillance Deptt., HR Management Deptt.. which helps us in highly efficient & Minimum Error work execution with maintaining Quality standards of ISO 9001, 14001 &18001. The same has also enabled us to achieve Gold Standard Award Winner in Worldwide participating International Quality Summit Awards 2015.
